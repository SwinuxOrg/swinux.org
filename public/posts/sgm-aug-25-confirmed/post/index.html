SGM Results Semester 2 2025

2025-08-01Swinux Committee 1 min read (166 words)
#event 

Congratulations to Swinux (the Swinburne Linux Hardware Code Club) for meeting qourum for Semester 2 2025!

Your votes have enabled the club to extend into Semester 2 of 2025. The club has nominated the following club members to the committee:

  • Timothy (Vice President)
  • Oscar (Treasurer)

They will join our President Dylan and Secretary Dani in the committee for Semester 2 of 2025. Congratulations to the candidates, and a thank you for everyone who ran for the committee. We appreciate everyone who helps contribute to keep the club running.

As elections are over, the committee’s next steps will be to progress work on several new club events and features which we hope will enhance your experience with the club. Likewise, if you have interesting ideas for club events please reach out to the committee, so that we can consider them in future.

If you have not yet signed up with club membership, you can do so via the channels below:
