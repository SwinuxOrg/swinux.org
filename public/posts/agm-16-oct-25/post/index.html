Annual General Meeting Announced: 4PM 16th of October 2025

2025-09-18Swinux Committee 1 min read (185 words)
#event 

This Year’s Annual General Meeting (AGM) date is confirmed, and will be held this Thursday 16th of October 2025 @ 4pm. It will be held online via Discord, and elections will be conducted using Rubric.

The AGM will establish the Swinux (Linux Hardware & Code Club) committee for 2026. Nominations for positions are open, closing Friday the 10th @ 5pm. Elections will be held for the Vice President role, with each candidate given time to give a statement before voting commences.

In order to vote, you must associate your student account with Rubric. This allows you to obtain a membership (free for Swinburne students) and ticket to attend the SGM, which is a requirement for voting. The committee encourages all who are interested in the club to attend and vote.

The club has exciting things in the planning stage for 2026 and beyond. By voting, you help to enable the club to create an environment for you, where your passions for everything Linux, Software and Hardware can be shared in a common space with like minds.
