Contact Swinux

Have you got interesting ideas for the club? Do you have something you want to talk to us about? Do you want to organise something cool? We want to hear from YOU!

We have multiple social media channels to engage with the club. However, if you want to get in contact with individual members, the best place to do so is through our Discord server. You can find the other avenues below:

  • Instagram: @swinuxclub

  • Discord: Swinburne Linux Hardware & Code Club

  • Club Page: Swinburne Linux, Hardware and Code Club

  • Email: contact@swinux.org

Please note the committee are students just like you, so we may take some time in responding. Don’t worry, we will get back to you!
