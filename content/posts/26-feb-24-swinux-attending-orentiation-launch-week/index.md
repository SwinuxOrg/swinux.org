**Swinux will attend Orientation and Launch Week for Semester 1 of 2026!**

Last week, the club announced that it would be attending launch week on the 25th and 27th of Feburary. Today, we are also confirming that we will be attending Launch week from the 2nd to the 4th of March. 

Orientation and Launch week will see the clubs of Swinburne represent themselves to new and returning Swinburne students. The Swinburne Student Association is also hosting free food and interactive activities to get people excited and connected going into Semester 1 of 2026.

Throughout the upcoming events, you will get a chance to meet club commitee members, talk about all things tech and learn more about the events and meetups the club are planning for 2026. Plus, the stall will also have Linux/Hardware/Coding themed goodies for you to collect!

If you're new to Swinux, you can have a look at our previous [events and meetings](https://swinux.org/tags/event) we've held. As always, we're keen to hear your feedback and input regarding the direction of the club. You can always get in contact with us through our email, contact@swinux.org, or alternatively via [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/swinuxclub/) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA).
