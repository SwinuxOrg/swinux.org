We have just announced the Learn to Code: Python Workshop for Semester 2 of 2025!

Learn the basics of coding with Python, a versatile programming language used in many industries around the world.

In this workshop you will code a small program, discovering some of the techniques and principles used in programming. This workshop will also de-obfuscate some of the common terms and expressions used by programmers to help strengthen your understanding of the field. By the end of the workshop you will have the tools necessary to start your own journey into the world of programming and code.

The workshop will start at 2:00 PM. Volunteers will assist attendees from 1:30PM with setting up the required software.

This workshop will cover the following major topics:

- How to Approach Coding
- Understanding Syntax
- Variables & Typing
- Conditions & Looping
- Making Functions
- Using Libraries

Attendance is in-person only, and this workshop will not be recorded. Materials used in this workshop will be provided online via https://swinux.org. Promotional photos may be taken during the workshop. If you do not wish to have your photo published online, please let a club volunteer know before or after the workshop.
