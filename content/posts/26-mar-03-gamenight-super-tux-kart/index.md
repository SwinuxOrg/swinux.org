The committee is pleased to announce FOSS Game Nights for Semester 1 of 2026.

FOSS Game Nights will showcase free, open source games that highlight the best of what the spirit of Open Source has to offer. We encourage everyone to join in the fun as we explore different types of games from various genres. It's an excellent opportunity to get together and socialise with fellow club members.

{{< inset-img src="images/tuxkartfoss.png" alt="Event details displayed in a visual format.">}}

On Thursday 12th of March @ 7PM, join the committee & club members of Swinux on Discord as we race to the finish line in *Super Tux Kart* in this inuagural event! 

*Super Tux Kart* is a fast paced racing game akin to *Mario Kart* featuring mascots from various Linux applications and packages which use power-ups and their unique racing styles to beat the competition. You can download *Super Tux Kart* for free from their main website below:

https://supertuxkart.net/Download

We'd like to measure attendance for this free event through Rubric, so keep an eye out for when the page goes live. We hope to see you there!

If you're new to Swinux, you can have a look at our previous [events and meetings](https://swinux.org/tags/event) we've held. As always, we're keen to hear your feedback and input regarding the direction of the club. You can always get in contact with us through our email, contact@swinux.org, or alternatively via [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/swinuxclub/) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA).
