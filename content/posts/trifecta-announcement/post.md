## The Swinburne Cybersecurity Club
The Swinburne Cyber Security Club is a student-led community for anyone passionate about cyber — from ethical hacking and digital forensics to networking, OSINT, and career pathways. [signup here](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=12093)

- Hosts & organises CTFs
- Runs hacking workshops
- Cybersec help & discussion

## The Swinburne Programming Club
We are a club all about programming, but also love other parts of Computer Science such as Cyber Security, AI/ML and even more. [signup here](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=14703)

- Hosts industry speakers
- Runs hackathon events
- Programming help & discussion

## The Swinburne Linux Hardware & Code Club
The Swinburne Linux, Hardware & Code Club (Swinux) is dedicated to providing an open forum for all students at Swinburne and beyond to share their knowledge, experiences and ideas in the Linux, Hardware and Code spaces. [signup here](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=12119)

- FOSS gaming nights
- Linux workshops
- Linux help & discussion

![Swinburne Event Details](/posts/trifecta-announcement/images/trifecta.png)

If you're new to Swinux, you can have a look at our previous [events and meetings](https://swinux.org/tags/event) we've held throughout the second half of 2025. As always, we're keen to hear your feedback and input regarding the direction of the club. You can always get in contact with us through our email, contact@swinux.org, or alternatively via [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/swinuxclub/) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA).

If you have not yet signed up with club membership, you can do so via the channels below:

- [Rubric Club Signup](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=12119)
- [Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA)
