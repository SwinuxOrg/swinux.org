Back again are our cherished game nights for Semester 1 of 2026!

The results are in and Hypersomnia has blown every contender out of the water!

It's a topdown shooter combining Counter-Strike and Hotline Miami. Originally released in Poland in 2023, but in development for 10 years, this Open Source game is playable on almost every platform you can think of including your browser.

Also of note is that several companies have adopted some of the technologies built for Hypersomnia, such as Snapchat, Ubisoft & Skydio. 

We encourage everyone to join in the vibrant chaos as we explore this unique top-down shooter. You can read more about (and play) the game on it's website:

https://hypersomnia.io/
