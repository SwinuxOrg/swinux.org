We have a special announcement and opportunity for students at Swinburne.

The Swinburne Rover Team are looking for students to join their Autonomous and Ground Control teams.

{{< inset-img src="images/RoverOSPromo.png" alt="Swinux branded poster containing the Swinburne Rover Logo and text described in this article.">}}

Working alongside existing team members, you can learn about and contribute to the development of their rover, which encompasses the following:
 - Working with Docker to deploy the rover's internal communication system.
 - Using a NVIDIA Jetson for compute as a hardware solution.
 - Utilising Linux-based ROS2 to control the rover.
 - Contribute to long-term projects such as a simulated testing environment.
 - Work on an [Open-Source ecosystem](https://github.com/SwinRoverTeam).
 - Enjoy a team-based collaborative environment.

If you're interested in joining, or would like to know more you can join their [Discord](https://discord.gg/rhQmBZwv22) or visit their [Website](https://www.swinroverteam.com/)

The Swinburne Rover Team are also hosting a ROS2 workshop tomorrow @ 11AM for students looking to learn more and get hands on with rover development. [You can get free tickets here](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?eid=67856).
