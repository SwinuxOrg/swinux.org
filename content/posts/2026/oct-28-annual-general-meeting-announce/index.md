We're happy to announce the Swinux Annual General Meeting has been organised pending confirmation from the Swinburne Student Association.

You'll get to meet some of the old and new committee members, and hang out with other club members as we pave the way to the new year. This will be a hybrid event, held in-person and via Discord. 

If you're still interested in running for any committee position, get in touch via contact@swinux.org or through @swinuxclub on Discord.

You can nominate yourself for the following positions:

- President
- Vice President
- Treasurer
- Secretary

Date: Wednesday 11th November
Time: 2:30 - 4:30 PM
Location: ATC327 or online via Discord
