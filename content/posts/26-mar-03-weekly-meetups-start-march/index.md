We're pleased to announce that our weekly meetups for Semester 1 of 2026 has been confirmed! Join us weekly every Tuesday from 4:30PM at EN405 to hang out with members from the Swinux, Cybersecurity and Programming clubs.

The weekly meetups are an excellent chance to develop your skills, get help with understanding concepts and technologies and socialise with different people. We encourage everyone from Computer Science to Engineering and everything in between to come along and share what they're working on and talk about what they've learnt in the tech space.

If you're new to Swinux, you can have a look at our previous [events and meetings](https://swinux.org/tags/event) we've held. As always, we're keen to hear your feedback and input regarding the direction of the club. You can always get in contact with us through our email, contact@swinux.org, or alternatively via [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/swinuxclub/) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA).


If you have not yet signed up with club membership, you can do so via the channels below:

- [Rubric Club Signup](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=12119)
- [Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/ZfCW8DhnhA)
